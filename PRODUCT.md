# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users buy and sell second-hand goods in Ethiopia: university students, young professionals, and families hunting for affordable phones, laptops, furniture, and appliances, plus local merchants and small business owners looking for inventory and reliable sellers. Buyers are often time-poor and wary — they want to find and compare trusted items fast instead of scrolling unstructured Telegram channels and Facebook groups. Sellers want to list quickly, price well, build a credible reputation, and field qualified buyers rather than repeating the same answers to strangers.

## Product Purpose

A structured, trustworthy marketplace that organizes buying and selling second-hand goods in Ethiopia, replacing unstructured social classifieds. It turns listing creation from a chore into a fast, guided flow (AI-assisted), surfaces transparent trust signals (seller trust score, verification, ratings, reports), and makes discovery quick through search, filters, categories, and similar listings. Success is measured by trust, speed, and simplicity on every screen: listing under 60 seconds, discovery under 30 seconds, and a mobile-first, highly accessible experience.

## Positioning

The fastest, most trustworthy way to buy and sell second-hand goods in Ethiopia — a structured marketplace instead of a classifieds feed. Every screen reinforces that this is where deals are found confidently.

## Brand Personality

Clear, warm, dependable. The voice is direct and helpful, never salesy or gimmicky; the interface feels like a competent, friendly assistant — it guides without getting in the way, reassures without overstating, and always shows its work (scores, statuses, empty states that teach).

## Anti-references

Must never look or feel like the messy status quo it replaces: unstructured social classifieds and group feeds — noisy cards, ad-hoc layouts, missing structure, cluttered grids, or copy that reads like a forum post. No template-boilerplate dashboards with no personality; no decorative overload, gradients for their own sake, or off-palette accents.

## Design Principles

- Trust first: every interaction increases buyer confidence — show scores, verification, and status transparently.
- Show, don't tell: state changes and results are visible and explained; empty states teach the next step.
- Fast by default: discovery and listing flows are shortened to seconds; hierarchy and search lead, not decoration.
- Consistent vocabulary: one component system (shadcn/ui), semantic tokens, and restrained color so listings stay the focal point.
- Accessible by design: WCAG AA contrast, 44px touch targets, visible focus states, motion that supports understanding rather than decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA is the commitment, per docs/04-design: minimum AA contrast, 44×44px minimum touch targets, a 2px primary focus ring on every interactive element, and motion limited to fade/scale/slide at 150–350ms with ease-out. Support reduced-motion preferences; never use color as the only indicator of status.
