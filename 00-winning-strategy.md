---

# Challenge Overview

The Ethiopian second-hand market is growing rapidly, but most transactions still occur through Telegram channels, Facebook groups, and informal social media posts.

This creates several problems:

- Poor discoverability
- No structured search
- Low trust between buyers and sellers
- Duplicate listings
- Time-consuming communication
- No seller reputation
- Poor listing quality

Rather than replacing these platforms, our product organizes the buying and selling experience into a structured, trustworthy, and modern marketplace.

# Problem Statement

## Current Situation

Today, buyers spend excessive time scrolling through unstructured Telegram messages and Facebook posts trying to find relevant products.

Sellers repeatedly answer the same questions, struggle to price their items, and have no reliable way to build credibility.

The result is a fragmented marketplace with low efficiency and low trust.

---

# Solution Statement

Our platform provides a structured marketplace where sellers can quickly create high-quality listings and buyers can discover trusted products through intelligent search, filtering, and seller reputation systems.

# Guiding Principles

Before implementing any feature, ask the following questions:

- Does this improve trust?
- Does this make buying faster?
- Does this simplify selling?
- Does this improve the user experience?
- Does this help us meet the judging criteria?

If the answer is **No**, the feature should not be included in the MVP.

# Demo Story

Our demonstration tells a simple story.

1. A seller uploads photos.
2. AI generates a complete listing.
3. The listing is published.
4. A buyer searches using filters.
5. The buyer reviews seller trust.
6. The buyer contacts the seller through Telegram or phone.
7. The seller receives an offer.

This demonstrates the complete marketplace lifecycle in under five minutes.
